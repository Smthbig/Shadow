## 🌑 The Shadow Philosophy: Friction over Force

Most app blockers are rigid—they lock you out, creating a cycle of frustration. **Shadow** takes a different path. We never truly block you; we just make your distractions "expensive" in time. 

By adding **intentionality breaths** (deliberate delays), Shadow forces you to pause and ask: *"Is this how I want to spend my time?"*

---

## ⚙️ The Core Engine

### ⚖️ Incremental Friction (The 70% Rule)
Friction shouldn't be all-or-nothing. 
*   **Safe Zone:** Use your apps freely until you hit 30% of your daily limit.
*   **Friction Zone:** Once 70% of your limit remains, Shadow begins adding incremental delays.
*   **Growth:** The wait time grows linearly from 0s up to a 10-second "Focus Breath" as you approach zero.

### 🔥 Launch Heat
Shadow tracks how "impulsive" you are. Rapidly attempting to re-open a distracting app increases its **Launch Heat**, adding extra penalty seconds to the delay. Slow down to cool the heat.

### 🛡️ Never Block (Autonomy)
You are the master of your device. When your time is up, you can add **unlimited +5m extensions**. But every extension comes with a cost: you must endure the full friction delay every time you open the app.

## ✨ Features

*   **🔍 Intentional Search:** No icons to tempt you. Type the app name to begin. Intention starts with a keystroke.
*   **⏳ Modular Doomsday Engine:** A visual grid on your home screen representing your Week, Month, Year, or a **Custom Day Count**. Watch the dots fill as your time passes.
*   **💎 Advanced Themes:** 
    *   **Glass Light/Dark:** Premium semi-opaque Material 3 aesthetic.
    *   **Transparent Light/Dark:** True transparency that lets your system wallpaper shine through with a soft UI glow.
*   **🎨 Shadow Backgrounds:** Choose from *Aurora Glow*, *Midnight Mesh*, *Distorted Mesh*, or *Classic Shadow*.
*   **📊 Productivity Insights:** A live dashboard showing your total **Shadow Interventions** today.
*   **⚡ Deep Focus Mode:** A toggle that **doubles all friction** for when you need maximum concentration.
*   **🤍 Shadow Whitelist:** Exempt essential apps (Phone, Maps, etc.) from all friction.

## 🛠 Setup & Requirements

1.  **Clone & Build:** `git clone https://github.com/smthbig/shadow.git`
2.  **Permissions:** 
    *   **Usage Access:** Required to track time and calculate friction.
    *   **Default Launcher:** Set Shadow as your home app for the full experience.
3.  **Optimization:** On Android 13+, you may need to enable "Allow Restricted Settings" in App Info to grant Usage Access.
