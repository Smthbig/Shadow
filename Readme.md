## 🌑 What is Shadow?

Shadow is not just another app blocker. It is a **Behavioral Friction Engine** disguised as a minimalist launcher. 

Most blockers are rigid—they shut you out, which often leads to frustration and "unblocking" loops. Shadow takes a different approach: **Friction over Force.** We never truly block you, but we make your distractions "expensive" in time.

## 🚀 The Core Philosophy: "Never Block"

Shadow believes in **user autonomy**. You are always in control, but every impulse comes with a cost:

- **Incremental Friction:** As you approach your daily limit, the delay to open an app grows. It starts fast and gradually slows down to a 10-second "intentionality breath."
- **Launch Heat:** The more frequently you try to open a distracting app, the more "heat" it generates, adding extra penalty seconds to the delay.
- **Unlimited Extensions (+5m):** Out of time? Add 5 minutes anytime. But be warned: you'll have to sit through the friction again.
- **Active Shadow:** If you're using an app and your time runs out, Shadow gently pulls you out and asks: *"Are you being intentional right now?"*

## ✨ Key Features

*   **💎 Real Glass UI:** A stunning, transparent aesthetic that lets your wallpaper breathe while keeping your focus sharp.
*   **📊 Productivity Insights:** See exactly how many "interventions" Shadow has made today to save your focus.
*   **⏳ Doomsday Clock:** A visual 7-dot grid on your home screen representing your week. Watch the dots fill as time passes—a subtle reminder of life's finitude.
*   **🔍 Intentional Search:** No app icons to tempt you. To open an app, you must type its name. Intention starts with a keystroke.
*   **🔒 Privacy First:** 100% local. No cloud, no tracking, no accounts. Your data stays on your device.

## 🛠 Setup

1.  **Clone & Build:** `git clone https://github.com/smthbig/shadow.git`
2.  **Permissions:** 
    - Enable **Usage Access** so Shadow can track your time.
    - Set Shadow as your **Default Home App**.
3.  **Configure:** Set your limits in Settings and choose your aesthetic (Glass Light, Shadow Dark, etc.).
